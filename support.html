Common questions

The screen turns off while I wear it. Wear mode keeps the screen awake while it is open. Make sure you tapped the kippa on the home screen to enter wear mode.

I bought Plus on another iPhone. Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore purchases". Purchases follow your Apple account.

How do I cancel Plus monthly? iPhone Settings, your name at the top, Subscriptions, iKippa Plus, Cancel.
