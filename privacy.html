Privacy Policy

iKippa for iPhone · Effective 5 September 2026

iKippa is designed to know nothing about you. This page explains exactly what the app does and does not do with data.

What we collect

Nothing. iKippa has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no servers of its own. The app does not collect, store, or transmit personal information.

What stays on your device

Your saved kippa designs and your settings (for example the wear-mode background) are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. They are included in your normal iCloud or iTunes device backup if you have backups enabled, and they are deleted when you delete the app. We never see them.

Purchases

iKippa Plus and single-kippa purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the app only whether a purchase is active. We do not receive your name, payment details, or address. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

iKippa asks for no system permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos. In wear mode it keeps the screen awake and may raise the screen brightness; both are restored when you leave wear mode.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of iKippa ever changes what it does with data, this page will be updated first and the effective date above will change.
