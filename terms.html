The free app

The classic velvet kippa and wear mode are free to use.

iKippa Plus

iKippa Plus unlocks all kippa styles and the kippa designer. It is offered in two forms:

  • Plus monthly, an auto-renewing subscription billed monthly.
  • Plus lifetime, a one-time purchase that unlocks Plus permanently on your Apple account.

A third option, One custom kippa, is a one-time purchase that lets you design and keep a single kippa without Plus. Each purchase adds one design.

Subscription terms
