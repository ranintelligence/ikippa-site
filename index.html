iKippa

Forgot your kippa? Put your phone on your head.

iKippa shows a kippa on your iPhone screen so you can put the phone on your head when you can't find a real one. Friday dinner, a quick blessing, a laugh with the family. It is a joke app, made with love.

The classic velvet kippa is free. iKippa Plus adds knitted, satin, Na Nach Breslov, Bukharian and suede styles, a designer for your own kippa with rim text and the classic metal clip, and an "Amen" when you tap it.
