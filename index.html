iKippa

Forgot your kippa? Put your phone on your head.

Not rabbinically approved. Probably.

Free · iPhone · iOS 17 or later

Point your camera here

iKippa draws a kippa on your iPhone screen, so you have one when you need one. Friday dinner, a quick blessing, a photo with the family, or the moment you realise you left yours in the other jacket. It is a joke app, made with love. It is not a religious article and satisfies no religious obligation.

What it does

Wear mode. Tap the kippa and it fills the screen, edge to edge. The display stays awake and turns up bright, so it reads as fabric from across the table. Tap it again and a voice says Amen.

Seven styles. Classic Velvet is free, forever. iKippa Plus adds Knitted with the little metal clip, Bar Mitzvah Satin, Na Nach Breslov, Bukharian, Suede and Star of David.

Design your own. Material, four to eight panels, colour, and text embroidered around the rim — a name, a date, a mazal tov. Hebrew reads right to left, the way it should.

Free to use. The classic velvet kippa, wear mode and the Amen sound cost nothing, forever.

iKippa Plus is $4.99, once. Every style and the full designer. No subscription, no renewal, nothing to cancel.
